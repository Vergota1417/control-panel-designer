# Electrical engineering and legal boundaries
THIS PROTOTYPE MUST NOT BE USED AS A CONSTRUCTION DRAWING.
The sample omits actual conductor sizes, overcurrent ratings, short-circuit-current ratings, protective bonding, neutral/return circuits, complete terminations, mechanical clearances, wiring method and environmental data. The illustrative voltage markings are not manufacturer-verified.
Engineering profiles (e.g., UL 508A, NFPA 70, NFPA 79, IEC 60204-1, IEC 81346) are applicability-dependent, edition-dependent and do not replace licensed standards documents, manufacturer data or qualified electrical/functional-safety assessment.
Any future conductor selection must account for circuit/load conditions, temperature, grouping, wiring method, terminal rating, overcurrent protection, fault conditions and the applicable jurisdiction and product standard.
A component's visual symbol, generic dimensions or internet picture cannot prove compatibility, ratings or safety.
Approval gates: architecture tests -> electrical design review -> real component datasheet verification -> physical design review -> commissioning and safety validation as applicable.
AI cannot sign off or certify safety-related logic or electrical assemblies.
